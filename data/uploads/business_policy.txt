Customer Onboarding Policy

New customers must complete account verification before receiving access to business systems. The operations team reviews submitted documents, validates business contact information, and confirms service requirements.

IT Support Escalation Process

If a ticket remains unresolved for more than 24 hours, the support analyst must escalate it to the operations lead. Critical production issues must be escalated immediately. Weekly ticket reports should include unresolved issues, assigned owners, and resolution timelines.

Meeting Follow-Up Process

After every business meeting, the project owner must document decisions, action items, blockers, and next steps. Action items should include owners and expected completion dates.

Process Improvement Guidelines

Teams should review recurring delays, duplicate work, unclear ownership, and manual handoffs. Recommended improvements should focus on automation, accountability, and measurable operational impact.
